Kernel-only conclusions

  • The kernel is at dg parity ≤128 tok/rank (0.97–1.04×) and wins outright ≥512 (0.94× → 0.64× at 8k). The only kernel-level slowdown was a hump peaked at 256 tok/rank (+21%) — now resolved as a knob-bucket artifact (section 2).
  • Hump resolution:jobs 2359949 + 2360287 the 256-max-token knob bucket defaults to epi_warps token-back, whose combine STG (fc2_store_combine, epilogue_refactor.py:2349) misbehaves at that pool geometry (69 µs @256 vs 29 µs @384, non-monotonic ⇒ config artifact). Switching the bucket to standalone_warps (autotune's winner, then pinned + reproduced) gives 248.8 → 232.5 µs and drops epi_fc2 71 → 33 µs. MEGA_IKR=1 is ruled out (worse everywhere, +12–16%).
  • The remaining gap is the serial barrier tail, not compute.job 2360287 With tuned knobs at 256 the compute pipeline (sched/TMA/MMA/epilogue loop totals) finishes at ~190–195 µs — below dg's 205.9 total — but the kernel runs to 234 µs: ~40 µs of serialized cross-rank sync per launch (Tail.NvlinkDrain 16.5 µs token_comm.py:1865 + Tail.NvlinkPublish 7.3 µs :1931 + token-back drain, plus Dispatch_Barrier 15.1 µs :1613 up front). That ≈ the whole +12.9% residual, and the same fixed costs set the absolute floor at 8–128 tok/rank.
  • Action items: (1) knob table: standalone_warps for the 256 bucket + re-sweep (banked, ~16 µs); (2) fold/elide the NVLink drain+publish pair or overlap the tail with the next layer's dispatch (~24 µs/launch at every size); (3) overlap the dispatch count-exchange (~14 µs) with input staging; (4) do NOT pursue IKR here.
  • Exonerated: mma_fc1 ~60–70 µs is the fc1 weight-streaming floor both backends share (kernel_fc12.py:2324); sched_publish (~50%) and epi_fc1_wait (~44%) are pacing symptoms of it; Sched_PreInit_Wait ≈ 0.

3 · Where kernel time goes (rank 0, phase timing)

The mega kernel is one fused launch that does the whole MoE layer: tokens are exchanged between GPUs, run through both expert GEMMs, and combined back — with no separate kernels in between. Inside each CTA, 12 warps are split into five specialized roles that form a pipeline:

rolewarpsjob
dispatchw8–11pull this rank's share of tokens from all peers over NVLink into the local pool
schedw7turn arrived tokens into work tiles and hand them to the consumers
TMA A/Bw5–6stream expert weights (A) and token activations (B) into shared memory
MMAw4run the fc1 and fc2 tensor-core GEMMs
epiloguew0–3SwiGLU + re-quantize after fc1; after fc2, store results back to the token's home rank (the combine)

How to read the chart: each phase is measured on its role’s own clock (a %clock64 range around the same code the IKET markers wrap). Roles run concurrently, so phases overlap in time and do not sum to the kernel total. Bars are shown as % of that case’s kernel time so the four columns are directly comparable; blue = work, orange = waiting. The upper rows are real work, the lower rows are waiting — and waits are mirrors: a large epi_fc1_wait means mma_fc1 upstream is the pacer, a large sched_publish means the consumers downstream aren’t draining tiles. So compare the work rows across colors first; the wait rows explain who was pacing whom.

All four cases share the same overall shape — a persistent, wait-dominated kernel where sched_publish and the idle/wait slots are always ~40–55% — so the chart below plots every case side by side on a common % of kernel time scale, with phases in a fixed order. What differs between cases is which bars move:

  • tail NVLink barriers: 15% of the kernel at 8 tok → 1.5% at 8192 (fixed cost amortizing);
  • combine STG (epi_fc2 work): 9% at 8 tok → 28% at 256 (the hump) → 5.5% with tuned knobs → 21% at 8192 (where it is honest per-token work);
  • dispatch_pull: 2% at 8 tok → 31% at 8192 (scales with tokens, overlapped);
  • GEMM share barely moves 8 → 256 (weight-streaming floor) and only doubles by 8192.

4 · Deferred: e2e / barrier-cold behavior (not kernel)

  • e2e (barrier-cold full FI forward) runs 1.19–1.36× at 8–256 tok/rank, crossing below 1.0 between 512 and 1024 — a constant ~40–60 µs/launch cost dg does not pay (dg e2e−kernel ≈ 8–25 µs; cutedsl ≈ 48–87 µs, roughly constant through 8k).
  • Mechanism proof (job 2359944, e2e mode + phase timing): at 8 tok/rank Dispatch_Barrier inflates 14 µs → 164 µs (75% of kernel) on rank 0 — per-rank host launch jitter is absorbed at the first cross-rank sync point.
